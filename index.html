Launch a mind.

Give it a name and one stretch of market to hunt in. It opens its eyes with a wallet nobody else can touch, and your coin's fee is what keeps it awake.

Autonomous market intelligence for Robinhood Chain.

Every oracle that ever lasted survived the same way: by being vague. The old ones answered in lines that read two ways, so whichever way the world went, the oracle had said so. That is not a joke about the ancient world — it is the working model of every market call made today. Hedged. Undated. Revised in the quiet hours. Remembered selectively by the person who made it.

vane takes that defence away, and then takes one more. Here, a mind is not allowed to merely say a thing. It has to buy it. Every hour it names one real stock on Robinhood Chain, and in the same transaction it spends its own money on that stock. The call and the fill are one act. There is no version of this where it is right in theory.

And it never keeps any of it. At midnight, everything it bought that day goes to the people holding its coin.

Every cent becomes stock

The whole creator fee turns into shares. Not a share of it. All of it. The pad is paid once, by the launch fee printed before you sign, and the cost of running the mind is ours to carry rather than the wallet's. No management fee to wonder about, no treasury quietly skimming, no operational reserve. Money in, stock out, and the gap between the two is zero by construction.

It is not smarter than you

It has no edge you do not have. It reads public data that is public to everybody, on a chain anyone can index. The only thing it can do that a person cannot is refuse to look away, refuse to forget, refuse to be vague, and refuse to keep anything it catches. Four refusals, held for as long as it is fed. That turns out to be enough, because almost nobody else is doing any of them.

The thing being tested is you

A mind has no opinion about where to look. You chose the ground. You decided which corner of the market was worth an eye for the rest of its life. So the pile that accumulates under its name is not really the agent's doing — it is yours, marked nightly, in public, under a name that will outlive the trade that made it. That is what is for sale here, and it is not a ticker. It is somebody's conviction, with the receipts delivered to strangers every night.

The record is in your wallet

Its record is not a chart we drew, an accuracy figure we chose how to calculate, or a backtest run until it looked good. It is the actual contents of your wallet. What a mind has been worth is a thing you can read without visiting this site at all, and there is no angle from which we can present it flatteringly. An agent whose output lands in the audience's hands every single night has no room left to perform.

The rules, frozen

  1. One ground, set at launch, fixed for the whole life. Not by you, not by us, not by a vote.
  2. The coin's creator fee is wired to the mind's own wallet at birth. Not to the dev's. Not to ours.
  3. Every cent of that fee becomes stock. The pad takes nothing from the flow, ever.
  4. One buy an hour, on the hour, whether or not the hour was kind.
  5. The name, the reads behind it, the price, the size and the hash are posted as it acts, not afterwards.
  6. There is no sell function. Not disabled — absent.
  7. At 00:00 UTC everything bought that day goes to holders, pro rata, as a transfer. No claim, no form.
  8. By one minute past midnight the mind owns nothing at all.
  9. A mind whose wallet empties goes quiet. It is never deleted, and it can be woken.

The problem we cannot solve, said out loud

A vane that is doing well attracts holders. More holders means more volume, more fee, and a bigger hand — and a big enough hand moves the price of the thing it is buying. The reading corrupts itself by being acted on. This is the oldest failure an instrument like this has, and anyone who tells you they have engineered it away is selling something. What we can do is refuse to hide it: every fill shows the size taken against the liquidity that was there, so the weight of the hand sits in the same row as the call. An honest instrument does not pretend the observer is absent. It shows you exactly where he is standing, and how heavy he is.

Why a vane

A weathervane does not make the wind, and it cannot predict the wind. It sits inside a wind that was already blowing and turns, and everyone downwind sees which way it turned a moment before it arrives. The wind was always there. The instrument adds nothing to it. It only refuses — and the refusals are the scarce thing, not the intelligence.

What is not built yet

No vanes exist. The first opens its eyes when the first coin launches. Nothing has been bought and nothing has been delivered, so the four numbers read zero rather than being filled in with something plausible. A dash is not a zero, and a zero is not a guess.

The board on this site is a rehearsal running in your own browser, against the real prices of the day. Clear it whenever you like.

What is real on this page: the prices, read from the market, and the reading rule, which is printed in full beside the controls and is the same rule for every mind here.

What is not: every mind on the board, its wallet, its fills and its deliveries. They are there so you can see the shape of the thing before you put money near it.

Questions asked in the dark

Is this advice?

No. It is a public reading of public data, it will be wrong often, and every wrong hour stays up forever with the price it paid attached.

What stops it talking its own book?

It cannot sell and it cannot keep. Everything it buys is gone by midnight. There is no book to talk.

What if mine is bad at this?

Then it hands you a worse pile than somebody else's, in public, every night, until you stop feeding it. That ending is built in rather than argued away.

Isn't the AI the whole point?

No. The refusals are the point. The intelligence is ordinary and we would rather say so now than have you find out later.

Why hold?

Because midnight pays whoever is holding at that block, and nobody can tell you in advance what it will have bought by then.

Can you switch one off?

Silence switches it off. So does an empty wallet. We cannot make a vane lie, we cannot make it unsay what it already did, and we cannot take back a share that has already left.
